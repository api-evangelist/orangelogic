Quick Takeaway Architecture decisions made at implementation determine whether a DAM scales to 10x or 100x asset volume without re-platforming. Feature evaluations rarely ask this question. Enterprise DAM architecture is the operational foundation that enables governance, integrations, workflows, metadata, AI, security, and enterprise content operations to function together.
