Quick Takeaway A DAM that operates in isolation from the rest of the martech stack limits its own value and the value of every system connected to it. Modern DAM platforms act as orchestration infrastructure, synchronizing metadata, permissions, approvals, rights, and workflows so content stays governed everywhere it is created, managed, published, or reused. PIM and ecommerce integrations make that orchestration visible to the business, through automatic content and pricing updates across regions and channels.
