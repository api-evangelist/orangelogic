For most organizations, a cloud-native SaaS DAM is the best default. It scales storage on demand, the vendor runs upgrades and uptime, and distributed teams and AI tools reach the same governed assets from anywhere. However, on-premise or private cloud makes sense when assets must sit in facilities you control.
