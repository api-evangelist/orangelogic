Quick Takeaway Digital rights management is an enterprise governance capability, not a standalone compliance function. Rights data that lives separately from assets, metadata, workflows, approvals, and distribution fails at enterprise scale. Enterprise rights governance affects content velocity, legal exposure, AI readiness, and operational efficiency.
